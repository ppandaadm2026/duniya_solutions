Privacy Policy

How Duniya Solutions handles information shared through this website and direct enquiries.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This website is operated by INFINITUM DUNIA SOLUTIONS INDIA LLP, which uses Duniya Solutions as its public-facing brand. This policy explains the information practices for this website.

1. Information collected through this website

The enquiry forms on this website do not transmit information to a website server or store submissions in a website database. When you submit a form, your browser opens your default email application and prepares an email addressed to contact@duniyasolutions.in. You choose whether to send it.

If you email or call us directly, we may receive contact details and the information you choose to share, such as your name, business contact details, organization, and enquiry information.

2. How we use enquiry information

  • To understand and respond to project, consulting, partnership, and training enquiries.
  • To communicate about a requested discussion, proposal, or service.
  • To maintain appropriate business correspondence and records.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell enquiry information. Information sent by email is processed by the email service providers and systems used by you and us. Website hosting, content delivery, and security providers may process technical request information as part of operating their services.

4. Retention and security

Business correspondence is retained only as reasonably required for responding to enquiries, managing business relationships, recordkeeping, or meeting applicable obligations. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, health information, or other sensitive data through an initial enquiry.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website build does not intentionally include advertising pixels or analytics tracking. Hosting or security infrastructure may process technical logs or essential data. See the Cookie Notice for more information. This notice should be updated if analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other tracking tools are added.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information contained in correspondence, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact contact@duniyasolutions.in or write to INFINITUM DUNIA SOLUTIONS INDIA LLP, CDA Sector-7, F/107, Cuttack, Odisha 753014.

This page is a general website privacy notice and is not a legal compliance certification. It should be reviewed against the company's actual systems and applicable law before publication.
