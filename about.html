About Duniya Solutions

Connecting business ideas with practical technology.

INFINITUM DUNIA SOLUTIONS INDIA LLP is a growing technology consulting, software development, and training business focused on building useful digital capabilities for organizations and professionals.

Who we are

Engineering-led. Business-focused. Learning-oriented.

Duniya Solutions brings together software application development, enterprise integration, database and Oracle capabilities, and technology enablement. Our approach is grounded in understanding the problem before selecting the tools.

We aim to work as a practical technology partner: helping clarify requirements, shape sound architecture, deliver maintainable solutions, and share knowledge with the teams who use them.

Our purpose
Make technology more useful, connected, and accessible.
